What does this tool do?

Requirement Analyzer examines a raw requirement and identifies ambiguity, missing information, assumptions, business rules, edge cases and testability concerns before the requirement moves into the delivery workflow.

Analyzer is the entry point. The delivery workflow starts with Requirement.
1 Requirement → 2 User Story → 3 Acceptance Criteria → 4 UAT Test Cases → 5 Test Data → 6 Process Flow → 7 Traceability → 8 BA Document
